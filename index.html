Self-learning: Microsoft Power Apps (Canvas App & Model-Driven Apps)

The purpose of this Canvas App is to build a lightweight, mobile-first Digital Shop-Floor Issue Tracking & Escalation System. It replaces paper forms, whiteboard logs, and verbal notifications with a direct digital pipeline between shop-floor machine operators and quality/maintenance engineers. Operating entirely on Canvas Apps, Power Automate, and Microsoft Lists, it accelerates response times for machine breakdowns, maintains digital traceability for ISO 9001 compliance, and keeps operational data synchronized in real time without needing complex enterprise database setups.

Power apps (Canvas Apps) 1 of 4

This video is to prove that there are no errors or warning signs in the Canvas Apps design.

Power apps (Canvas Apps) 2 of 4

Provides a clean landing screen where users choose their operational path upon opening the app. Operators can quickly jump to data entry, this reduce extra clicks on the factory floor. Optimized for mobile phones or handheld tablets right beside manufacturing machinery. Operators can quickly select part/tool numbers, type failure descriptions and upload photos.

Power apps (Canvas Apps) 3 of 4

The engineer dashboard is password-protected. Once unlocked, it displays a searchable and filterable gallery of all pending or active tickets. Engineers can search by Part Number, Tool Number, or ID, and filter by status badges. Tapping any issue card to store the ticket in app memory and seamlessly open the detailed screen. The engineer can read the complete record with dynamic headers, full operator notes and the attached photo of the failed part. Engineers use this screen to enter root-cause analyses and assign engineering remarks.

Power apps (Canvas Apps) 4 of 4

Aggregates raw list data into key visual metrics and status counters (e.g. Total Open Issues, Issues by Priority). Maintenance engineers use this screen to spot recurring machine bottlenecks (such as frequent downtime on Press 03), evaluate team response times and allocate maintenance resources effectively.

Power apps (Model-Driven Apps)

This Model-Driven Apps is included Business Process Flow (BPF). It tracks and enforces the lifecycle stages (Logging ⇒ Investigation ⇒ Maintenance ⇒ Resolution) specifically for whichever individual ticket record is currently open, enabling supervisors to pinpoint operational delays and reallocate engineering personnel efficiently. It will trigger an automated notification (Teams and Outlook) when the Business Process Flow (BPF) reaches Stage 3: Maintenance. The Management Dashboard functions as the central executive command center for plant managers, maintenance leads and quality directors. Delivers instant, aggregated visibility into total open issues, active critical line stops and ticket volume across production lines without requiring manual shift reports. The interactive KPI charts (such as Issues by Machine or Failure Category Breakdown) to identify recurring mechanical failures on specific tooling or presses before major breakdown events occur.
